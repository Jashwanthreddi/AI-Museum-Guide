Title: Getting Here

The museum is easily accessible by both road and rail.

Rail Access:
The major railway stations, Kachiguda and Nampally, are located within a three-mile radius of the museum.

Road Access:
Frequent road transport buses are available from all parts of the city to Afzalgunj, which is within walking distance of the museum.

Title: Transportation to the Museum

Secunderabad Railway Station:
You can reach Afzalgunj by taking any of the following bus routes: 1, 1C, 1J, 1P, 2, 2C, 2J, 2U, 2V, 2Z, 7Z, 8A, 8C, or 8M.
Afzalgunj is within walking distance of the museum.

Nampally Railway Station:
You can reach Afzalgunj via bus routes: 8M, 8R, 8U, 9, 9D, 9E, 9F, 9K, 9C, 9M, 9Q, 9R, 9X, 9Y/F, 65, 65M, or 65S.
Afzalgunj is within walking distance of the museum.

Mahatma Gandhi Bus Station (MGBS):
The museum is within walking distance from MGBS.

Jubilee Bus Station:
Take bus route 8R to reach Afzalgunj. The museum is a short walk from Afzalgunj.

Title: Location

The Salar Jung Museum is located on the southern bank of the river Musi. It is near several important monuments of the old city. The historic Charminar, Mecca Masjid, High Court, State Central Library, and Osmania General Hospital are all located within a one-mile radius of the museum.

---

Title: How to Reach Salar Jung Museum

The museum is easily accessible by both road and rail. The major railway stations—Kachiguda and Nampally—are within three miles. Public buses run frequently from all parts of Hyderabad to Afzalgunj, which is within walking distance of the museum.

---

Title: At the Museum

The Salar Jung Museum houses 39 galleries spread across three buildings: the Central Block, the Eastern Block (Mir Laiq Ali Khan Bhavan), and the Western Block (Mir Turab Ali Khan Bhavan).  
- The Central Block contains 26 galleries (13 on the ground floor and 13 on the first floor).  
- The Western Block has 7 galleries.  
- The Eastern Block includes 6 galleries.

In addition to the galleries, the museum has several support sections, including:  
- Education Wing  
- Chemical Conservation Laboratory  
- Photo Section  
- Display Section  
- Reception  
- Sales Counter

Security is managed by the Central Industrial Security Force (CISF).

---

Title: Facilities for Visitors

The museum offers various amenities for visitors:
- Seating arrangements at important locations
- Wheelchairs and restrooms for those in need
- Public cloakrooms and restrooms at key locations
- A cafeteria operated by the Telangana Tourism Department

---

Title: Publications

To assist scholars, researchers, and interested visitors, the museum has published several catalogues covering its collections. These include catalogues on:
- Persian manuscripts
- Arabic manuscripts
- Urdu manuscripts
- Special monographs
- SJM Bi-Annual Research Journal

Popular leaflets and picture postcards are also available at the HHEC Souvenir Shop on the ground floor of the Central Block.

---

Title: Facilities for Scholars

The museum has a rich library that includes a wide range of printed books and thousands of manuscripts in Persian, Arabic, and Urdu. Scholars may use the library by obtaining passes at the reception counter.

Title: Entry Fee

- ₹50 per adult (above 18 years) for Indian citizens  
- ₹20 per child (above 5 years) for Indian citizens  
- ₹500 per person for foreign visitors  
- ₹50 per still camera or smartphone (mobile) for photography  

Additional Notes:
- Audio guide ticket is free with a mobile ticket.  
- Government school children (up to 14 years) are allowed free entry upon showing a valid ID card and an authorization letter from the school.  
- A fine of ₹500 will be charged for unauthorized photography.
